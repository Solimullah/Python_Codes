# Bug Report: Incorrect item count in shopping cart

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Add three different items to the shopping cart.
2. Navigate to the shopping cart page.
3. Observe the item count in the cart summary.

**Severity:** Medium

**Expected Result:**
The item count should be 3.

**Actual Result:**
The item count is 1.
